The caption says the curve is built "later (Plate 49)". In this edition that is the run of square-grid Koch plates, 49 to 55, and the curve is Plate 51's: each step is replaced by eight steps a quarter as long. Every stage doubles the length, and the dimension is log 8 / log 4 = 3/2.

Page 32 draws the fourth stage, 4,096 steps, traced from right to left. Below it is the first third of the third stage. Both match this generator step for step.

The sausage

Pick a radius ε and draw a disc of that radius around every point of the curve. The union of the discs is the band, Minkowski's sausage. Its area divided by its width 2ε is the curve's length as seen at resolution ε. For a smooth curve that number settles to the true length; for this one it keeps growing like ε1−D = ε−1/2.

The critical stage

Mandelbrot's point on page 32: the band changes while the construction adds detail larger than ε, and stops changing once the new detail is smaller. Here, a stage's new bumps are its step size, 4−n of the base. Turn on the highlight and step through the stages. The changed area shrinks to slivers once 4−n falls below ε.

Why the estimate wobbles

The local slope does not sit still at −1/2. It swings a little each time ε shrinks by a factor of four, because the curve's structure repeats at exactly that ratio. Fitting across a full swing averages it out: stage 6 gives about 1.49.
